### chunk:solar-pv
Solar photovoltaic (PV) panels convert sunlight directly into electricity using the photovoltaic effect in semiconductor cells, typically made of silicon. Output is direct current, which an inverter converts to alternating current for the grid.

### chunk:solar-capacity
A solar panel's rated capacity is measured in watts under standard test conditions. Actual output depends on irradiance, temperature, and the angle of the sun, so panels produce the most energy near solar noon on clear days.

### chunk:solar-storage
Because solar generation stops at night, solar systems are often paired with battery storage so that energy captured during the day can be used after sunset. Battery storage also smooths short dips caused by passing clouds.
